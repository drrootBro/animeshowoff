The anime portrait that keeps your face

An anime portrait should be a portrait first and anime second. Ours starts from your real face — and refuses to let go of it.

Search for an anime portrait today and you will find hundreds of filters that do the same disappointing trick: they paste a generic anime face over yours, borrow your hair colour, and call it a transformation. The result is cute — and it is a stranger. People who have tried those apps say the same thing in their reviews: it doesn't look like me. An anime portrait that could be anybody is just clipart with your haircut.

AnimeShowoff was built around the opposite rule: the person is locked, the style is free. Your facial structure, your expression, your eye colour, your hair colour and your skin tone are treated as untouchable anchors. A broad laugh stays a broad laugh — it is not softened into a polite smile. A calm, closed-mouth expression is not inflated into a grin. Grey strands stay grey. Deep brown eyes stay deep brown. What changes is everything around those anchors: the outfit, the styling, the rendering, the world you are standing in.

The launch lineup has three anime portrait looks. Anime Braids is the signature: a photorealistic portrait of you in a sailor-style outfit with twin braids woven from your own hair colour — the closest thing to stepping into an anime frame as yourself. Star Eyes renders you as a polished anime illustration with starlit highlights in the eyes, while keeping your age lines and expression honestly in frame. Shoujo goes softest — a pastel watercolour wash in the classic girls'-manga style — with the same four anchors (expression, eyes, hair, skin) held under lock so the softness never turns into somebody else.

Every look is produced twice, and the version that keeps you most recognisably you is the one delivered. You never see the reject pile and never have to choose between candidates — picking the best frame is the factory's job, not yours. Portraits come in one-time packs of 12, 36 or 60, with no subscription and no expiring credits.
