Questions

Will it actually look like me?

That is the whole point of AnimeShowoff. Your face, expression, eye colour, hair colour and skin tone are locked in — the styling changes, you don't. Every look is generated twice and our system delivers the version that keeps you most recognisably you. Look at the test wall above: original on the left, results after it.

How does it work?

Upload your photo, pick your looks — or select all — and receive your portraits. There is nothing to tune and no candidates to sort through: the best of each pair is chosen for you automatically.

Is it a subscription?

No. Packs are one-time purchases — 12, 36 or 60 portraits — and they are yours forever. No credits that expire, no monthly fee.

What happens to my photo?

Your uploaded photo is used only to make your portraits and is deleted within 24 hours. Only upload a photo of yourself, or one you have permission to use.
